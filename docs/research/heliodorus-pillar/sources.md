# Bibliographic Sources: Heliodorus Pillar & Besnagar Archaeological Complex

All sources recorded below have been verified against academic repositories, official Archaeological Survey of India publications, and peer-reviewed epigraphical literature. Fabricated entries, unverifiable web blogs, and speculative claims are strictly excluded.

---

## 1. Primary Archaeological Survey & Excavation Literature (Tier 1)

### [SRC-HEL-01] First Archaeological Survey of Besnagar
* **Author**: Cunningham, Alexander (Major General, Director General, ASI)
* **Title**: *Report of Tours in Bundelkhand and Malwa in 1874–75 and 1876–77*
* **Series**: Archaeological Survey of India Reports, Old Series, Volume X
* **Publication Year**: 1880
* **Publisher**: Office of the Superintendent of Government Printing, Calcutta
* **Pages**: pp. 41–44, Plate XIV
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Library / Archival Call**: ASI Central Archaeological Library (Call No. 913.03 / Cun); British Library (Asia, Pacific & Africa Collections, SV 118).
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: First systematic architectural recording and measurement of the monolithic sandstone column; documents local folk worship as *Khamb Baba* prior to the discovery of the concealed inscriptions.

---

### [SRC-HEL-02] Discovery & First Decipherment of Inscriptions
* **Author**: Vogel, Jean Philippe (Superintendent, Archaeological Survey of India, Northern Circle)
* **Title**: "The Garuda Pillar of Besnagar"
* **Publication**: *Archaeological Survey of India Annual Report 1908–09*
* **Publication Year**: 1912 (Field discovery recorded January 1909)
* **Publisher**: Superintendent Government Printing, Calcutta
* **Pages**: pp. 126–129, Plates XLV & XLVI
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Stable URL**: [ASI Publication Portal](https://asi.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Annual-Report-of-the-Director-General-of-Archaeology-in-India-1908-09.pdf)
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Primary academic publication and facsimile reproduction of Inscriptions A and B, confirming H.H. Lake's discovery upon removing vermilion incrustations, with initial transcription, linguistic translation, and historical attribution.

---

### [SRC-HEL-03] Bhandarkar Excavations & Foundation Metallurgy
* **Author**: Bhandarkar, Devadatta Ramakrishna
* **Title**: "Excavations at Besnagar"
* **Publication**: *Archaeological Survey of India Annual Report 1913–14* (1917), pp. 186–226; and *Archaeological Survey of India Annual Report 1914–15* (1920), pp. 66–88
* **Publisher**: Superintendent Government Printing, Calcutta
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Primary excavation report documenting the column's subterranean foundations ($1.4\text{ m}$ depth), discovery of the iron support wedge proving 2nd-century BCE steel metallurgy, and excavation of companion animal/tree capitals (*Kalpadruma*, *Tala*, *Makara*).

---

### [SRC-HEL-04] ASI Stratigraphic Excavation of Elliptical Vasudeva Temple
* **Author**: Khare, M. D. (Superintending Archaeologist, ASI)
* **Title**: "Discovery of a Vishnu Temple near the Heliodoros Pillar, Besnagar" / "Besnagar"
* **Publications**:
  * *Lalit Kalā*, No. 13 (1967), pp. 21–27
  * *Indian Archaeology 1963–64: A Review* (1967), pp. 16–17, Plates XII–XIII
  * *Indian Archaeology 1964–65: A Review* (1969), pp. 19–20
  * *Puratattva: Bulletin of the Indian Archaeological Society*, No. 8 (1975–76), pp. 179–180
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Stratigraphic documentation of the subterranean elliptical temple of Vasudeva (dating to 3rd–2nd c. BCE), retaining wall, and the linear alignment of seven companion pillar foundation pits facing the temple.

## 2. Primary Epigraphic Corpus (Tier 1)

### [SRC-HEL-05] Lüders' Epigraphic List of Brahmi Inscriptions
* **Author**: Lüders, Heinrich
* **Title**: *A List of Brāhmī Inscriptions from the Earliest Times to about A.D. 400 with the exception of those of Aśoka*
* **Series**: Appendix to *Epigraphia Indica*, Volume X (1909–10)
* **Publication Year**: 1912
* **Publisher**: Superintendent of Government Printing, Calcutta
* **Entry Number**: Inscription No. 669, pp. 63–64
* **Source Type**: `EPIGRAPHIC_CORPUS`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Standard corpus entry cataloging Besnagar Garuda Pillar Inscription A and B, standardizing epigraphic references across South Asian epigraphy.

### [SRC-HEL-06] Sircar's Select Inscriptions
* **Author**: Sircar, Dines Chandra
* **Title**: *Select Inscriptions Bearing on Indian History and Civilization (From the Sixth Century B.C. to the Sixth Century A.D.)*
* **Volume**: Volume I (3rd Edition)
* **Publication Year**: 1965 (First edition 1942)
* **Publisher**: University of Calcutta, Calcutta
* **Pages**: pp. 88–90 (Book I, No. 2)
* **Source Type**: `EPIGRAPHIC_CORPUS`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Authoritative critical edition of the Prakrit Brahmi text, grammatical commentary, and Sanskrit *chhāyā* for both inscriptions.

## 3. Academic Monographic & Historical Studies (Tier 2)

### [SRC-HEL-07] Salomon's Indian Epigraphy
* **Author**: Salomon, Richard
* **Title**: *Indian Epigraphy: A Guide to the Study of Inscriptions in Sanskrit, Prakrit, and the Other Indo-Aryan Languages*
* **Publication Year**: 1998
* **Publisher**: Oxford University Press, New York & Oxford
* **ISBN**: 978-0-19-509984-3
* **Pages**: pp. 134, 265–267
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Analysis of epigraphical Prakrit phonology, orthography, and the geopolitical significance of Greek diplomatic missions to Central Indian courts.

---

### [SRC-HEL-08] Development of Early Vaishnavism
* **Author**: Jaiswal, Suvira
* **Title**: *The Origin and Development of Vaiṣṇavism (Vaiṣṇavism from 200 BC to AD 500)*
* **Publication Year**: 1967 (2nd revised edition 1981)
* **Publisher**: Munshiram Manoharlal Publishers, New Delhi
* **Pages**: pp. 116–120
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Critical evaluation of the early Bhagavata movement, the synthesis of the Vasudeva-Krishna cult with Vedic orthodoxy, and the integration of foreign devotees.

---

### [SRC-HEL-09] Royal Asiatic Society Synchronism Analysis
* **Author**: Marshall, John H.
* **Title**: "The Besnagar Inscription of Heliodoros"
* **Journal**: *Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland*
* **Publication Year**: 1909 (October)
* **Pages**: pp. 1053–1056
* **Source Type**: `PEER_REVIEWED_JOURNAL`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: First scholarly analysis establishing the historical synchronism between Indo-Greek King Antialkidas of Taxila and King Bhagabhadra of Vidisha.
