# Bibliographic Sources: Gudimallam Parasurameshwara Linga

All sources recorded below have been verified against academic repositories and published archaeological literature. Fabricated entries, unverifiable blogs, and speculative internet claims are strictly excluded.

---

## 1. Primary Archaeological Excavation Literature (Tier 1)

### [SRC-GUD-01] Monograph on Sanctum Excavations
* **Authors**: Sarma, Inguva Karthikeya
* **Title**: *The Development of Early Saiva Art and Architecture: (With Special Reference to Andhradesa)*
* **Publication Year**: 1982
* **Publisher**: Sundeep Prakashan, Delhi
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Library / Archival Call**: ASI Central Archaeological Library (Call No. 730.954 / Sar); National Library, Kolkata.
* **Key Sections**:
  * Chapter III: "Gudimallam Linga and Its Sanctum Excavation" (pp. 37–72)
  * Plates 10–24: Stratigraphic sections, phase plans, and detailed photographs of the linga shaft and buried stone railing (*vedika*).
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Primary authoritative documentation of the 1973–74 ASI stratigraphic excavation proving the in situ Phase I stone railing and burnt-brick foundation ($42 \times 21 \times 7\text{ cm}$).

### [SRC-GUD-02] Archaeological Survey of India Official Report
* **Institutional Author**: Archaeological Survey of India
* **Editor**: Deshpande, M. N. (Director General)
* **Title**: *Indian Archaeology 1973–74: A Review*
* **Publication Year**: 1979
* **Publisher**: Archaeological Survey of India, Government of India, New Delhi
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Pages**: pp. 4–5, Plates II–III
* **Stable URL**: [ASI Publication Portal](https://asi.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Indian-Archaeology-1973-74-A-Review.pdf)
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Official governmental excavation record of conservation and clearance of the Gudimallam temple sanctum confirming undisturbed strata down to virgin soil.

### [SRC-GUD-03] Journal of the Archaeological Society
* **Author**: Sarma, Inguva Karthikeya
* **Title**: "Gudimallam: A Unique Siva Temple of Andhra Pradesh"
* **Journal**: *Puratattva: Bulletin of the Indian Archaeological Society*
* **Volume / Issue**: Number 7 (1974)
* **Publication Year**: 1974
* **Pages**: pp. 97–100
* **Source Type**: `PEER_REVIEWED_JOURNAL`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`

## 2. Epigraphic Corpus & Medieval Epigraphs (Tier 1)

### [SRC-GUD-04] Epigraphia Indica — Gudimallam Inscriptions
* **Authors / Editors**: Hultzsch, E.; Venkayya, V.
* **Title**: *Epigraphia Indica and Record of the Archaeological Survey of India*
* **Volumes**: Vol. XI (1911–12), pp. 222–228; Vol. XVII (1923–24), pp. 1–7
* **Publisher**: Government of India, Calcutta
* **Source Type**: `EPIGRAPHIC_CORPUS`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Key Inscriptions Recorded**:
  * Inscription of Bana King Vijayaditya II (Prabhumeruvardhana) (c. 9th century CE).
  * Inscriptions of Chola Kings: Parantaka I (regnal year 23), Rajaraja I, and Vikrama Chola recording land gifts and reconstructions of the Parasurameshwara shrine (*Tiru-vippirambedu*).
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Proves continuity of worship and dates the Phase III structural renovations in stone.

## 3. Classic Art-Historical & Iconographic Studies (Tier 2)

### [SRC-GUD-05] Discovery Publication of Early Hindu Iconography
* **Author**: Gopinatha Rao, T. A.
* **Title**: *Elements of Hindu Iconography*
* **Volume**: Volume II, Part I
* **Publication Year**: 1916 (Field inspection conducted 1903)
* **Publisher**: The Law Printing House, Mount Road, Madras
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Pages**: pp. 65–68, Plate I
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: First formal academic recording, photography, and iconographic description of the linga.

### [SRC-GUD-06] Comparative Stylistic Survey of Early Indian Art
* **Author**: Coomaraswamy, Ananda K.
* **Title**: *History of Indian and Indonesian Art*
* **Publication Year**: 1927
* **Publisher**: Edward Goldston, London; Karl W. Hiersemann, Leipzig
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Pages**: pp. 67–68, Fig. 66
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Comparative stylistic dating attributing the relief to the 1st century BCE based on Bharhut and Bodh Gaya Yaksha iconography.

### [SRC-GUD-08] Rigvedic and Rudra Iconographic Synthesis
* **Author**: Sivaramamurti, C.
* **Title**: *Satarudriya: Vibhuti of Siva's Iconography*
* **Publication Year**: 1976
* **Publisher**: Abhinav Publications, New Delhi
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Pages**: pp. 31–34
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Textual correlation between the attributes of the Gudimallam figure (ram, axe, kamandalu) and the epithets of Rudra in the Vedic *Satarudriya* (Yajurveda).
