# Bibliographic Sources: Sanchi Great Stupa & Archaeological Complex

All sources recorded below have been verified against official academic repositories, Archaeological Survey of India publications, and epigraphical corpora. Speculative online claims, non-peer-reviewed blog posts, and unverified attributions are strictly excluded.

---

## 1. Primary Archaeological Excavation & Restoration Monographs (Tier 1)

### [SRC-SAN-01] First Survey & Reliquary Excavations
* **Author**: Cunningham, Alexander (Major, Royal Engineers; Later Director General, ASI)
* **Title**: *The Bhilsa Topes; or, Buddhist Monuments of Central India: Comprising a Brief Historical Sketch of the Rise, Progress, and Decline of Buddhism; with an Account of the Opening and Examination of the Various Groups of Topes around Bhilsa*
* **Publication Year**: 1854
* **Publisher**: Smith, Elder and Co., London
* **Pages**: pp. 180–297, Plates XIX–XXXIII
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Library / Archival Call**: British Library (System number: 000839446); ASI Central Archaeological Library (Call No. 913.03 / Cun).
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: First systematic survey and excavation monograph of the Sanchi monument cluster; documents the opening of Stupa 2 and Stupa 3 and the primary discovery of the inscribed relic caskets of Sariputra and Maha-Maudgalyayana.

---

### [SRC-SAN-02] Definitive Archaeological Survey & Conservation Monograph
* **Authors**: Marshall, Sir John (Director General of Archaeology in India); Foucher, Alfred; with epigraphy by Majumdar, N. G.
* **Title**: *The Monuments of Sāñchī*
* **Volumes**: 3 Volumes (Vol. I: Texts and Inscriptions; Vols. II & III: Plates and Architectural Drawings)
* **Publication Year**: 1940
* **Publisher**: Government of India Press, Calcutta / Arthur Probsthain, London
* **Pages**: Vol. I: pp. 1–396; Vol. II: Plates 1–70; Vol. III: Plates 71–141
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Stable URL / Archival**: ASI National Archive of Monuments; Digital Library of India.
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Definitive comprehensive archaeological monograph documenting the 1912–1919 clearances, stratigraphy, structural phases, complete torana relief catalogs, and the epigraphic corpus of over 800 inscriptions.

---

### [SRC-SAN-03] Archaeological Survey of India Official Guide
* **Author**: Marshall, Sir John
* **Title**: *A Guide to Sanchi*
* **Publication Year**: 1918 (3rd revised edition 1955)
* **Publisher**: Manager of Publications, Government of India, Delhi
* **Pages**: pp. 1–168, Plates I–XV
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Authoritative structural breakdown of the three building periods of the Great Stupa (Ashokan brick core, Shunga casing/balustrades, and Satavahana gateways).

---

### [SRC-SAN-04] ASI Post-Independence Site Monograph
* **Author**: Mitra, Debala (Director, Archaeological Survey of India)
* **Title**: *Sanchi*
* **Series**: Archaeological Survey of India Site Guides
* **Publication Year**: 1957 (4th edition 1965, reprinted 1978)
* **Publisher**: Archaeological Survey of India, New Delhi
* **Pages**: pp. 1–74, Plates I–XII
* **Source Type**: `EXCAVATION_REPORT`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Concise archaeological analysis of the Buddhist stupa typology, chronological phasing, Gupta additions, and monastic layout.

## 2. Primary Epigraphic Corpora & Edict Editions (Tier 1)

### [SRC-SAN-05] Epigraphia Indica — Corpus of Sanchi Inscriptions
* **Author / Editor**: Bühler, Georg
* **Title**: "The Inscriptions of Sanchi"
* **Journal / Series**: *Epigraphia Indica and Record of the Archaeological Survey of India*
* **Volume**: Volume II (1894)
* **Pages**: pp. 87–116, 366–408
* **Publisher**: Superintendent of Government Printing, Calcutta
* **Source Type**: `EPIGRAPHIC_CORPUS`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: First comprehensive critical edition and paleographic classification of early Brahmi donative inscriptions on the Great Stupa stone balustrades.

---

### [SRC-SAN-06] Lüders' Epigraphic List of Sanchi Inscriptions
* **Author**: Lüders, Heinrich
* **Title**: *A List of Brāhmī Inscriptions from the Earliest Times to about A.D. 400 with the exception of those of Aśoka*
* **Series**: Appendix to *Epigraphia Indica*, Volume X (1909–10)
* **Publication Year**: 1912
* **Publisher**: Superintendent of Government Printing, Calcutta
* **Entries**: Nos. 161–668, pp. 26–63
  * Inscription No. 345: Vidisha Ivory Carvers Inscription (p. 36)
  * Inscription No. 346: Satavahana King Siri Satakarni Inscription (p. 37)
* **Source Type**: `EPIGRAPHIC_CORPUS`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Standardized numbering and comparative catalog of all early historic Brahmi inscriptions at Sanchi, establishing the baseline references used in global South Asian scholarship.

---

### [SRC-SAN-07] Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum — Ashokan Pillar Inscription
* **Author / Editor**: Hultzsch, Eugen
* **Title**: *Inscriptions of Asoka*
* **Series**: Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum (CII), Volume I (New Edition)
* **Publication Year**: 1925
* **Publisher**: The Clarendon Press for the Government of India, Oxford
* **Pages**: pp. 160–161, Plate VIII
* **Source Type**: `EPIGRAPHIC_CORPUS`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_1_PRIMARY_EXCAVATION_EPIGRAPHY`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Authoritative critical edition, transliteration, English translation, and facsimile of the Sanchi Ashokan Pillar Schism Edict (*Sanghabheda Edict*).

## 3. Academic Monographic & Art-Historical Studies (Tier 2)

### [SRC-SAN-08] Visual Narratives and Gateway Iconography
* **Author**: Dehejia, Vidya
* **Title**: *Discourse in Early Buddhist Art: Visual Narratives of India*
* **Publication Year**: 1997
* **Publisher**: Munshiram Manoharlal Publishers, New Delhi / Columbia University Press, New York
* **ISBN**: 978-81-215-0736-3
* **Pages**: pp. 91–138 (Chapter 5: "Sanchi: The Great Stupa")
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Rigorous narrative and semiotic analysis of the aniconic representations of the Buddha, Jataka relief sequences, and compositional techniques across the four toranas.

---

### [SRC-SAN-09] Monastic Archaeology and Relic Cult
* **Author**: Schopen, Gregory
* **Title**: *Bones, Stones, and Buddhist Monks: Collected Papers on the Archaeology, Epigraphy, and Texts of Monastic Buddhism in India*
* **Publication Year**: 1997
* **Publisher**: University of Hawaii Press, Honolulu
* **ISBN**: 978-0-8248-1870-8
* **Pages**: pp. 23–43, 86–98
* **Source Type**: `ACADEMIC_MONOGRAPH`
* **Reliability Tier**: `TIER_2_PEER_REVIEWED_ACADEMIC`
* **Evidentiary Contribution**: Methodological analysis of Sanchi's donative epigraphy demonstrating active monastic ownership, non-clerical financial participation, and the institutional centrality of the corporeal relic cult.
